FAQ

Good questions before you buy.

Is Tendrite Pro a subscription?

No. Tendrite Pro is a one-time license for Pro workspace capabilities. Tendrite Cloud, if you choose it later, is a separate monthly service for the managed relay.

Can I use the phone companion without Tendrite Cloud?

Yes. Pro supports private connectivity through Tailscale or a relay you operate yourself.

Can I verify my license offline?

Yes. Tendrite verifies the signed license locally after activation. Initial activation and later refreshes need an internet connection.

What does the Cloud beta include?

Early testers can try the account-backed entitlement, device inventory, and managed relay flow while it is being hardened for public availability.
